⏰ Los 12 Tiempos Verbales

▼
En inglés existen 12 tiempos verbales que combinan 3 tiempos (presente, pasado, futuro) con 4 aspectos (simple, continuo, perfecto, perfecto continuo).

🟢 PRESENTE 4 tiempos

Presente
Simple
S + V (+s/es 3ª p.)
She works every day.
Presente
Continuo
S + am/is/are + V-ing
She is working now.
Presente
Perfecto
S + have/has + V-pp
She has worked here.
Presente
Perfecto Continuo
S + have/has been + V-ing
She has been working all day.

🔵 PASADO 4 tiempos

Pasado
Simple
S + V-ed / irregular
She worked yesterday.
Pasado
Continuo
S + was/were + V-ing
She was working at 8pm.
Pasado
Perfecto
S + had + V-pp
She had worked before.
Pasado
Perfecto Continuo
S + had been + V-ing
She had been working for hours.

🟣 FUTURO 4 tiempos

Futuro
Simple
S + will + V
She will work tomorrow.
Futuro
Continuo
S + will be + V-ing
She will be working at 8pm.
Futuro
Perfecto
S + will have + V-pp
She will have worked by then.
Futuro
Perfecto Continuo
S + will have been + V-ing
She will have been working for 3 hours.

🔀 Estructuras Condicionales

▼
Los condicionales expresan situaciones hipotéticas y sus consecuencias. Hay 5 tipos principales en nivel B2.
ZERO
If + present simple, present simple
If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.Verdades universales.
FIRST
If + present simple, will + V
If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.Posibilidad real.
SECOND
If + past simple, would + V
If I won the lottery, I would travel the world.Hipotético.
THIRD
If + had + V-pp, would have + V-pp
If I had studied, I would have passed.Pasado irreal.
MIXED
If + had + V-pp, would + V
If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now.Pasado → presente.

🔄 Voz Pasiva

▼
La voz pasiva se usa cuando el objeto de la acción es más importante que quien la realiza.

📐 Fórmula general

Objeto + be (conjugado) + V-participio + (by + agente)

🎯 Pasiva en los principales tiempos

Present Simple: The letter is written by John.La carta es escrita por John.
Past Simple: The house was built in 1990.La casa fue construida en 1990.
Present Perfect: The work has been done.El trabajo ha sido hecho.
Future: The project will be finished tomorrow.El proyecto será terminado mañana.
Modal: It must be done today.Debe ser hecho hoy.

💪 Verbos Modales

▼
Los modales expresan habilidad, posibilidad, obligación, permiso o consejo.

📋 Tabla de modales

CAN / COULDHabilidad
MAY / MIGHTPosibilidad
MUSTObligación fuerte
SHOULDConsejo
WILL / WOULDFuturo / cortesía
OUGHT TODeber moral
HAVE TOObligación externa
BE ABLE TOHabilidad específica

⚠️ Diferencias clave B2

must vs have toMust = obligación interna. Have to = obligación externa.
mustn't vs don't have toMustn't = prohibido. Don't have to = no es necesario.
